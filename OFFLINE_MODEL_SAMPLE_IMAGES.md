# Offline Model Sample Image Guide

Use this as a presentation checklist for the TensorFlow Lite classifier in FloraLife. For best accuracy, use one clear leaf, bright light, sharp focus, and minimal background clutter.

## Apple

- `apple apple scab`: Apple leaf with dark circular scab patches on a plain background.
- `apple black rot`: Apple leaf with brown-to-black lesions and clear dead tissue edges.
- `apple cedar apple rust`: Apple leaf with vivid orange rust spots.
- `apple healthy`: Clean green apple leaf with no spotting or halos.

## Blueberry

- `blueberry healthy`: Smooth blueberry leaf with even green color and no lesions.

## Cherry

- `cherry including sour powdery mildew`: Cherry leaf with obvious white powder patches.
- `cherry including sour healthy`: Bright cherry leaf with no powdery coating.

## Corn / Maize

- `corn maize cercospora leaf spot gray leaf spot`: Corn leaf with long gray rectangular lesions.
- `corn maize common rust `: Corn leaf with many small raised orange rust pustules.
- `corn maize northern leaf blight`: Corn leaf with large cigar-shaped gray lesions.
- `corn maize healthy`: Clean green corn leaf with no rust or blight.

## Grape

- `grape black rot`: Grape leaf with circular brown lesions and tiny black specks.
- `grape esca black measles`: Grape leaf with tiger-striping and scorched tissue.
- `grape leaf blight isariopsis leaf spot`: Grape leaf with multiple brown blotches.
- `grape healthy`: Green grape leaf with no striping or dark lesions.

## Orange

- `orange haunglongbing citrus greening`: Orange leaf with blotchy asymmetric yellow mottling.

## Peach

- `peach bacterial spot`: Peach leaf with many small dark spots and yellow halos.
- `peach healthy`: Smooth green peach leaf with no shot-hole spotting.

## Pepper Bell

- `pepper bell bacterial spot`: Pepper leaf with many small dark angular spots.
- `pepper bell healthy`: Pepper leaf with even green tone and no dark spotting.

## Potato

- `potato early blight`: Potato leaf with brown concentric ring spots and yellow haloing.
- `potato late blight`: Potato leaf with large dark water-soaked blight patches.
- `potato healthy`: Healthy potato leaf with no target spots or wet blight patches.

## Raspberry

- `raspberry healthy`: Raspberry leaf with crisp green leaflets and no scorch or spotting.

## Soybean

- `soybean healthy`: Soybean leaf with smooth green surface and no lesion clusters.

## Squash

- `squash powdery mildew`: Squash leaf with obvious white powdery patches.

## Strawberry

- `strawberry leaf scorch`: Strawberry leaf with dark spots and scorched brown-red margins.
- `strawberry healthy`: Bright green strawberry leaf with no scorch or dead spotting.

## Tomato

- `tomato bacterial spot`: Tomato leaf with many tiny dark bacterial spots and light yellow haloing.
- `tomato early blight`: Tomato leaf with brown target-ring lesions and yellow haloing.
- `tomato late blight`: Tomato leaf with large dark wet blight patches and collapsed tissue.
- `tomato leaf mold`: Tomato leaf with yellow patching and visible moldy underside if possible.
- `tomato septoria leaf spot`: Tomato leaf packed with many small round septoria spots.
- `tomato spider mites two spotted spider mite`: Tomato leaf with dense yellow stippling and light bronzing, ideally with webbing.
- `tomato target spot`: Tomato leaf with larger ringed target-like brown lesions.
- `tomato tomato yellow leaf curl virus`: Tomato top leaf with upward curl and bright yellowing.
- `tomato tomato mosaic virus`: Tomato leaf with mottled mosaic green-yellow coloring and slight distortion.
- `tomato healthy`: Tomato leaf with strong even green color and no ring spots, curl, or mildew.

## Background

- `background`: Single centered leaf only, sharp, bright, and filling most of the frame to avoid this class.
